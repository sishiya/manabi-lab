このアプリについて

顕微鏡でのぞいた海の水の一滴です。青緑のつぶは藍藻（らんそう）。光と、水に溶けた栄養塩で、どんどん分かれて増えます。生き物はそれを食べてためると2つに分かれ、そのたびに少しだけ変わります。だれも「よい個体」を選びません。エサにありついて分かれられた者だけが子を残します。

部品は3つ

  • 細胞: 丸い体。大きさ。
  • 節: 細胞や、ほかの節の先から生える棒。長さ・向き・筋のふれ幅・リズムのずれ。
  • 感覚: 感じる範囲と、エサへ・小さい相手へ・大きい相手から 向かう強さ。

決まりは6つ

  1. 水: 小さな生き物はすぐ止まる。節を動かすと、水の抵抗で体が動く。海の水はゆっくり渦を巻いて流れ、なにもかも運ぶ。一滴は、上下の面が目の細かい網の箱。生き物は出られず、水と栄養塩と小さな藍藻だけが海と入れかわる。底の砂つぶは、水を通さない。生き物はふれると表面にそってすべり、しばらく押しつけられると上をこえる。すきまには小さな者だけが入れる。
  2. 打つ: 節の先は、うしろへ振るときだけ水を強くかく。
  3. 曲がる: 体の前の左右で感じたにおい（藍藻・自分がのみこめる相手・自分をのみこむ相手）の差の向きへ曲がる。においは水ににじんで広がる。
  4. 食べる: 細胞の食べる所（くぼみ。はじめはななめ前）・節の先にふれた、それより小さいものを食べる。のみこんでいる間は次を食べない。
  5. 使う・作る: 重いほど、速く打つほど使う。食べた物の3割は消化できず、うしろからふんとして捨てる（細菌に分解されて栄養塩にもどる）。使った分は水に溶けて出す。
  6. 分かれる: 体をもう1つ作れるだけためたら分かれる。節が生える・のびる・左右にそろう・なくなる。飢えて死ぬと体はバラバラになり、かけらもエサになる。ふれた同じ種類の仲間と、くっついて遺伝子を混ぜることもある（接合。群体に入っていない単細胞だけ。数はふえず、30秒ほど泳げない。混ぜた遺伝子は次に分かれるときから。混ぜるかどうかも遺伝子しだい）。食べた藍藻を消化せずに飼うこともある（中でも光で作って半分を渡し、ゆっくり分かれる。葉緑体のはじまり）。分かれた子が、くっついたまま離れないこともある泳ぎ方も遺伝子で変わる: エサのにおいの中でゆるめる・のみこむ間は休む・前でぶつかると下がる・砂つぶにくっついて止まり、水を流してエサを待つ（襟鞭毛虫の柄。群体ごと止まることもある＝海綿の道）。（群体。何細胞まで1つに保つかも遺伝子で、それより先の子は1つの細胞で離れて新しい群体を始める。細胞は寒天質でとなりどうしつながって向きがそろい、打つ力は足し合わさり、かたまりが大きいと口に入らない）。群体の仲間が感じたことを、合図として受けとることもできる。群体の中では、仲間に囲まれた内の細胞と外の細胞で、遺伝子の出し方を変えられる（内は打たずに休む・外は分かれずに仲間へ渡す・ためた分を分けあう・節を外だけ／内だけに作る＝細胞の種類）。

はじまりと、本物とのちがい

はじまりの生き物は、すべての真核生物（動物・植物・菌・原生生物）の最後の共通祖先、LECA（約15〜20億年前）にならっています。鞭毛は2本、細胞の中にはもうミトコンドリアがあり、ときどき仲間と遺伝子を混ぜます。その前の、古細菌が細菌を取りこんでミトコンドリアにした出来事は、1回しか起きておらず、くわしいことがまだわかっていないので、ここでは描きません。

タイトルと右上の ☰ の「途中からのぞく」では、進化の節目の一滴（群体が育ったころなど）から見られます。どれも、はじまりの2本毛から同じ決まりで何時間も回して保存したものです。

食べた物は口から体の中へ入り、食胞（膜の袋）になって、体の中をめぐりながら消化され、小さく茶色っぽくなって消えます（膜の輪のある緑の点は藍藻、琥珀の丸はほかの生き物を狩ったとき。輪のない明るい緑の点は、消化せずに飼っている藍藻。どれも外の藍藻と同じ大きさ）。上の「平均 N 代」は、いまいる生き物がはじまりから何代目かの平均です。進化は時間ではなく代で進みます。

右上の図鑑には、進化の節目の形（狩る者・群体・多分裂など）がはじめて現れたときに、その形と時刻を自動で写します。下に出る「はじめて」の知らせを押しても開きます。

一滴の上で右クリック（スマホは長押し）すると、砂つぶを落とせます。外から運ばれてきた砂つぶとして、ほかの砂と同じに扱います。

本物より速くしている所があります。一滴の数百匹で海の何百万匹のかわりをするため、形の突然変異は3倍。何億年もかかる変化を、時間を縮めて見せています。縮めるのは時間だけで、藍藻と原生生物が分かれる速さの比、ウイルスや大きな捕食者に殺される割合は本物に合わせています。緑の点1つは、藍藻の細胞1つではなく小さなかたまりです。食べる所は、LECA にならって前からななめ横のくぼみ（腹の溝）から始まり、場所も遺伝子で変わります。

栄養塩はまわりの海から一滴のふちへしみこみ、ゆっくり広がります。藍藻は光が強いほど速く増えます。藍藻も海から流れこみます。泳ぎ方・触手・狩る者は決まりにありません。部品の組み合わせから生まれます。

About this app

This is a drop of seawater seen through a microscope. The blue-green specks are cyanobacteria (blue-green algae). With light and the nutrients dissolved in the water, they divide and multiply. The creatures eat them, and when they have stored enough they split in two, changing a little each time. Nobody picks the “best” ones. Only those that find food and manage to divide leave young.

3 parts

  • Cell: a round body, with a size.
  • Segment: a rod growing from the cell or from the tip of another segment. Length, angle, how far the muscle swings it, and the timing of its beat.
  • Senses: how far it can sense, and how strongly it turns toward food, toward smaller prey and away from bigger ones.

6 rules

  1. Water: tiny creatures stop at once. Moving a segment pushes on the water, and the body moves. Seawater flows in slow swirls and carries everything along. The drop is a box whose top and bottom are fine mesh: creatures cannot get out, but water, nutrients and small algae swap with the sea. Sand grains on the bottom let no water through. A creature that touches one slides along its surface, and after pressing on it for a while climbs over. Only small ones fit into the gaps.
  2. Beat: a segment tip pushes the water hard only when it swings backward.
  3. Turn: a creature turns toward the side where it smells more on the left or right of its front (algae, things small enough to swallow, things that could swallow it). Smells spread through the water.
  4. Eat: it eats smaller things that touch its mouth (a groove, at first toward the front-side) or the tips of its segments. While swallowing, it does not eat the next one.
  5. Use and make: being heavier and beating faster use more energy. A third of what it eats cannot be digested and is left behind as waste (bacteria break it down into nutrients again). Used energy dissolves into the water.
  6. Divide: when it has stored enough to build a second body, it divides. Segments can sprout, grow longer, pair up left and right, or disappear. A creature that starves falls apart, and the pieces become food too. It may stick to a touching partner of the same kind and mix genes (mating; only single cells not in a colony; their number does not grow, they cannot swim for about 30 s, and the mixed genes are used from the next division; whether it mixes also depends on its genes). It may keep eaten algae alive instead of digesting them (they make food with light inside and hand over half; they divide slowly; the start of chloroplasts). Its young may stay stuck to it (a colony; how many cells it keeps together is also in its genes, and young beyond that leave as a single cell to start a new colony; cells are joined to their neighbors by jelly, so they face the same way, their beats add up, and a big clump does not fit in a mouth). How it swims also changes with its genes: slowing down in the smell of food, resting while swallowing, backing up when bumped in front, or sticking to a sand grain and pulling water past itself to wait for food (like the stalk of a choanoflagellate; a whole colony can stop like this, the road to sponges). A colony can take what its members sense as a signal. In a colony, inner cells surrounded by others and outer cells can use their genes differently (inner cells rest without beating, outer cells pass food to the others instead of dividing, stored food is shared, segments grow only outside or only inside: kinds of cells).

The beginning, and how this differs from the real thing

The first creature is modeled on LECA, the last common ancestor of all eukaryotes (animals, plants, fungi and protists), about 1.5 to 2 billion years ago. It has 2 flagella, already has mitochondria inside, and sometimes mixes genes with others. The event before that, when an archaeon took in a bacterium that became the mitochondrion, happened only once and is still not well understood, so it is not shown here.

With “Look in partway” on the title screen and in the ☰ menu, you can watch a milestone drop (such as when colonies have grown). Each was run for hours from the first two-flagella cell under the same rules, then saved.

Food goes in through the mouth and becomes a food vacuole (a small bag of membrane). It circles inside the body while it is digested, shrinking and turning brownish until it is gone (a green dot inside a thin ring is algae, an amber dot is a hunted creature; bright green dots without a ring are algae kept alive, not digested; all algae are drawn the same size as those outside). “avg. gen N” at the top is the average number of generations since the beginning for the creatures alive now. Evolution moves by generations, not by time.

The Guide at the top right records a milestone shape (hunters, colonies, multiple fission and more) with its time, the first time it appears. You can also open it by tapping the “First” message at the bottom.

Right-click on the drop (long-press on a phone) to drop a grain of sand. It is treated like the other sand, as a grain carried in from outside.

Some things run faster than in real life. A few hundred creatures in one drop stand in for millions in the sea, so shape mutations are 3 times as common. Changes that took hundreds of millions of years are shown with time squeezed. Only time is squeezed: how fast algae and protists divide compared with each other, and how many are killed by viruses and by bigger predators, follow the real numbers. One green dot is a small clump of algae, not a single cell. The mouth starts, as in LECA, as a groove on the front-side (the ventral groove), and its place also changes with the genes.

Nutrients soak in from the sea at the edges of the drop and spread slowly. Algae grow faster in stronger light, and some also flow in from the sea. Swimming styles, tentacles and hunters are not in the rules. They come from combinations of the parts.
